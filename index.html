About Me

I'm a Unity developer with expertise spanning AR/VR/MR development, 2D/3D games, 3D simulations, and embedded systems integration. My passion lies in creating immersive, interactive experiences that push the boundaries of what's possible in Unity.

From industrial training simulations to engaging games, from augmented reality applications to hardware-integrated VR experiences, I've worked across the full spectrum of Unity development. I specialize in Meta Quest platforms and have deep experience with embedded system integration using ESP32 and various sensor technologies.

My development philosophy emphasizes clean architecture, maintainable code, and building systems that scale. I leverage advanced Unity tools like DOTween, Odin Inspector, and ScriptableObjects to create designer-friendly workflows and robust, production-ready applications.

Whether it's crafting realistic physics simulations, implementing complex multiplayer systems, or integrating IoT devices with Unity experiences, I bring technical expertise and creative problem-solving to every project.
